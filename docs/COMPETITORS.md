# Similar services (researched 2026-10-17)

Framing: Closer is not a matcher. Xero JAX, Leapfin and Airwallex's own accounting integrations match transactions to invoices automatically. Closer is the step after that: for each item left over it fixes the mismatch type, applies amount limits, and puts money-moving actions behind one approval card signed with a separate approver key. We do not compete on match rate.

Question: who already solves "match Airwallex money movement to an invoice ledger and handle the mismatches", and what does Closer have that they do not?
Method: 8 source types tried; result per type below. Search summaries were checked by opening the page where marked "opened".

## Reading
- Airwallex's accounting integrations (rows 1-3) and Xero JAX (row 4) already sync and match, Xero at a stated 60%+ of lines. Closer is built to work on what they leave. The claim "nobody does reconciliation" is false and is not made, and no match-rate comparison is made. The Builder Guide sentence is about starter kits for builders, not about Airwallex products.
- The defensible claim is narrower: after automatic matching, the decision on each leftover item is typed, bounded by amount limits in code, separated from approval (a card signed with a separate approver key), and replay-safe (0 extra executions in 20 replays), and the repo shows it. Verified absence: the official AgentOS skills list (5 skills) has no reconciliation skill (opened by the red team 2026-10-28).
